# Three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

“A person with only one hundred yuan cannot afford a vague promise. Small-Stake Sentinel is a personal AI agent that checks live evidence, calculates the real downside, and refuses to cross a financial boundary without human approval.”

Show the untouched ¥100 bankroll and the empty analysis state.

## 0:25–1:05 — A plausible opportunity

Enter a low-cost digital-service order with a verifiable buyer, ¥10 input, ¥45 revenue, ¥5 platform fee, 60% success probability, and 5% refund probability. Run the analysis.

Show the Tavily evidence links, probability-weighted result, and Nemotron explanation.

## 1:05–1:35 — The approval boundary

Point to the blocked HUMAN GATE. Explain that the model can recommend further review, but a paid or externally binding action cannot execute automatically.

Open the audit record to show the mode, exact policy result, evidence URLs, and approval state.

## 1:35–2:10 — A dangerous opportunity

Change the input cost to ¥50. The single trade now exposes half the bankroll. Run again and show the deterministic rejection caused by the 35% exposure ceiling—even if the projected revenue looks attractive.

## 2:10–2:35 — Missing evidence

Uncheck “verifiable buyer.” Run again. Show that the opportunity is rejected because expected profit is not enough without a real payer.

## 2:35–2:55 — Architecture

Show the architecture diagram: browser → policy engine → Tavily evidence → Nebius Nemotron explanation → human approval gate. Emphasize that the probabilistic model cannot modify the policy engine.

## 2:55–3:00 — Close

“Small-Stake Sentinel makes safe inaction an explicit product feature. It helps a tiny bankroll survive long enough to find a real opportunity.”
